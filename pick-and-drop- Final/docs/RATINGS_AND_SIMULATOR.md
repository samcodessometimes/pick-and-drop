# Customer ratings and the demo order simulator

## Ratings
- After an order is DELIVERED, the customer sees "How was <shop>?" on the order page: five stars and an optional comment (300 characters).
- One rating per order, only by the customer who placed it, only after delivery. Enforced in the database by `rate_order()` (migration 07), not in the page.
- Nobody can insert, edit or delete a rating directly. A shop's average and count are recalculated from the real ratings every time.
- Shop cards and shop pages show stars, the average and the count. A shop with no ratings shows grey stars and "New". No rating is ever invented.
- Who can read ratings: the customer who wrote it, that shop's staff, and admins.
- Needs `db/migrations/07_ratings.sql` run once in Supabase. Until then the order page shows "Could not check your rating" and the cards show "New".

## Delivery PIN
Generated by the database when the order is placed. The customer sees it on the order page. The rider must enter it to deliver; the database refuses DELIVERED without it.

## Simulator
Admin, Orders, "Simulate customer orders (demo)". It places 1, 3 or 5 orders through the same `place_order` function the customer checkout uses, from DEMO shops only, with names like "Demo Customer 412" and "(simulated)" addresses. Each result has a "View as customer" link, which shows the order page and its PIN. The orders then go through the real flow: merchant accepts, admin assigns a rider, rider delivers with the PIN, customer rates.
The orders belong to the admin account that pressed the button, so use that same admin login to view them as a customer.
